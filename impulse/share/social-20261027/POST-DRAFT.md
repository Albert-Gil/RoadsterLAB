RoadsterLAB has a new look—and now you can see more of the physics while it happens.

Three cars launch together. One relies on electric drive, one adds fan suction,
and one adds cold-gas thrust.

The differences go beyond the finish line:

• A live force budget shows what is limiting acceleration.
• A friction circle explains why the same steering can produce a different corner.
• Thrust, weight and remaining air connect the hop to the landing burn.
• Contact loads change as the platform turns upside down.

I’ve also rebuilt the interface around the simulation, added a director camera,
improved the lighting and shadows, and made the controls easier to use on a phone.

Change a parameter. Make a prediction. Run it. Follow the plots.

Have some fun experimenting with it:
https://albert-gil.github.io/RoadsterLAB/

Still in development. What would you add next? Suggestions and bug reports are welcome.

Independent exploratory model; illustrative inputs, not Tesla performance data.
