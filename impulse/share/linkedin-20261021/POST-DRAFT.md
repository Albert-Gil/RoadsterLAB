Tesla says its new Roadster can defy gravity. I built a physics simulator to explore how.

Over the past week, I’ve been exploring what happens when you add fan suction and
cold-gas thrusters to a car.

How does the grip change? What happens to acceleration? How quickly does the air
tank empty? And can the remaining air bring the car back down gently?

That curiosity became RoadsterLAB: an interactive physics lab where you can change
the parameters, run the experiments and watch the car, forces and plots respond.

Launch. Corner. Rotate. Hop.

The video shows the motion. The plots help explain it.

It’s an independent exploratory model built around illustrative assumptions,
not an official Tesla simulator or a prediction of the production car.

Have some fun experimenting with it:
https://albert-gil.github.io/RoadsterLAB/

It’s still in development. What would you add next? Suggestions and bug reports
are very welcome.
