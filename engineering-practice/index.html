About this practice bank

150 original, single-answer questions for practicing engineering first principles in English. The order and answer positions change between sets. Numerical answers are approximate where stated.

The set mixes compute cooling, pumps and piping, heat transfer, structures, cryogenics, methane processing, troubleshooting and thermal-cycle selection. Thirty questions include a graph or schematic.

Cycle-selection scenarios state their constraints. No cycle is presented as universally optimal. These are practice scenarios, not an official SpaceX question bank or a disclosed Starbase process design.

Progress is saved in this browser on this device. It does not automatically synchronize between your phone and computer. Answers are locked after selection so the first response counts.
